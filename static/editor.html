Language & direction

A webtoon is drawn as one long strip and served as tiles cut every so many pixels - through balloons, through faces, through the middle of the typesetting. When a chapter arrives looking like that it is joined back up and cut again in the gaps between panels. Turn this off to keep the tiles exactly as they came.

Pages come out near the first number, cut at the nearest gap between panels. Every cut is a gap: where there is none the page runs on to the next one however far away it is, rather than being cut through the artwork. Any page that ends up past the second number is named for you afterwards.
